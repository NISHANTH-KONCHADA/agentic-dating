# PRODUCT: "Agentic Dating"
Every person is represented by an AI agent. The agent reads ONLY two sources about its person: (1) their public LinkedIn, (2) their public Instagram. Nothing else. The agents then date each other on their people's behalf, and every person gets a ranked list of who fits them best.

4. Agents ACTUALLY DATE: real multi-turn conversations between two agents, viewable/streamed live in the UI. Not just scores.

# AGENT ARCHITECTURE (this is what gets graded most — make it excellent)

## Stage 1 — Reader/Analyst (per person)
Input: normalized LinkedIn JSON + Instagram JSON. Output strict JSON `PersonProfile`:
- summary (2-3 sentence vibe read), one-liner tagline
- needs: [{need, why, evidence, confidence}] — what they likely want in a partner/life (e.g., ambition-matching, adventure, stability, creative collaboration)
- hobbies: [{name, evidence, source}]
- interests: [{topic, depth: casual|serious|obsessed, evidence}]
- values, communication_style, energy (introvert/extrovert spectrum), lifestyle (travel, fitness, nightlife, food, pets, city vs nature), career_drive, humor_style, social_style, aesthetic
- deal_breakers_guess (only if strongly evidenced), green_flags, conversation_hooks (5 things they'd love to talk about)
- persona_card: how this person talks (tone, slang, emoji use, sentence length) — derived from captions/about text — so their agent can speak in their voice
- data_quality: {linkedin: 0-1, instagram: 0-1, notes}
Do it in 2 passes: (a) extraction of facts with evidence, (b) synthesis into needs/qualities. Show the raw evidence in the UI.

## Stage 2 — Pre-screen (all pairs)
25 people = 300 pairs. Fast cheap compatibility pass per pair (structured rubric: values alignment, lifestyle overlap, ambition compatibility, interest overlap, complementarity, communication fit, mutual-need satisfaction — computed from BOTH directions, since A's fit for B ≠ B's fit for A). Cache everything. Run with concurrency limit + rate-limit handling.

## Stage 3 — The Dates (the star feature)
For each person, take their top ~5 pre-screen matches → ~60-90 unique pairs get a FULL DATE:
- Two agents, each with: its person's PersonProfile + persona_card + strict rule "you only know what's in your own profile; you know nothing about the other person except what they say on the date". This makes the dates real, not omniscient.
- Setting: randomly assigned date scenario (coffee shop, rooftop dinner, bookstore walk, arcade, hike, etc.) with 1-2 lines of scene-setting.
- 8-10 turns alternating, natural, in each person's voice, with curiosity, disagreement, humor, chemistry. Agents must probe each other's needs, not just exchange facts. No sycophancy — agents should be honest when there's friction.
- After the date each agent independently outputs a JSON verdict from its person's POV: {would_see_again: bool, chemistry 0-10, values_match 0-10, needs_met: [..], friction_points: [..], best_moment (quote), one_line_review, final_score 0-100}.
- Store full transcript + verdicts. Stream turns live via SSE in the UI.

## Stage 4 — Ranking
For each person: final_score for candidate X = weighted blend of (their own post-date score for X 50%, X's score for them 25%, pre-screen 25%), with a mutual-match bonus when both would_see_again. Rank everyone (for people with no full date, use pre-screen only and mark "predicted"). Each ranking row shows: score, mutual/one-sided badge, "why you fit" (2 lines), "friction", and a link to the date transcript. Also compute a global "best couple" leaderboard.
